SkinAI

An iOS app for keeping track of the skincare products you own. Scan a product, review its ingredients, compare it with your routine, and save it to your skincare fridge.

how it's built

 ╭───────────────────────────╮
 │  iphone app               │  swift · swiftui · swiftdata
 │  apple vision scans here  │  works offline, no account
 ╰────────────┬──────────────╯
              │
              ▼
 ╭───────────────────────────╮      ╭──────────────────────╮
 │  node.js backend          │ ───▶ │  supabase            │
 │  cleans + dedupes data    │      │  postgres · auth     │
 ╰────────────┬──────────────╯      │  private backups     │
              │                     ╰──────────────────────╯
              ▼
 ╭───────────────────────────╮
 │  google gemini            │  key stays on the server
 │  explains ingredients     │  rate limited · cached
 ╰───────────────────────────╯
  • on-device first: your fridge and routines live on your phone
  • product import cleans up names and ingredient lists and skips duplicates
  • private by design: only product name, brand and ingredients go to gemini, after a heads-up
  • manual backup, plus undo and “recently removed”
