Melville / Case study02 — Idea & goal

Idea & goal

I worked at Brandy Melville and wanted to recreate the store in Roblox. I used the layout, displays, and materials I knew from work as a starting point.

The brand already had a strong following and a recognizable style. The goal was to build a store where players could browse, try on, and buy clothing.

Melville / Case study03 — References

References

Brandy Melville stores use wood shelving, white paneled walls, wicker baskets, and closely packed clothing displays. Flags, college graphics, and spot lighting give the spaces a consistent look.

These references guided the shelving, display density, and wall details in my Roblox build.
