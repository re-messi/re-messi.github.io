Melville / Case study02 — Idea & goal

The starting point

A place I already knew.

From my everyday surroundings to a world you can step into.

I worked at Brandy Melville, so the store was part of my everyday life. I wanted to recreate a place I knew by heart, down to the crowded racks, baskets, and warm wood.

Brandy’s almost cult-like following made the idea especially interesting: there was already an audience who knew its look and would recognize those details. My goal was to bring that familiar feeling into Roblox, giving people a space to wander, try things on, and spend time in.
