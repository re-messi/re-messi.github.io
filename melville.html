Why I made it

Brandy Melville has a cult following, and I wanted to bring the feeling of its stores into Roblox. My goal was to make the experience immersive, so walking through it would feel like being in an actual Brandy Melville store.

How I built it

I built the store myself in Roblox Studio, using its tools to put the environment together.

Meshes in Blender
I made meshes in Blender to recreate realistic items you would find in the actual store.
Textures and normal maps
I used normal maps to add surface detail to walls, tables, and other items.
AI-assisted mesh generation
For some items, I used a photo as a reference for AI mesh generation to get a close match to the original object.
Interactions
I coded the mirrors, try-on feature, purchasing, and doors to make the store interactive.
