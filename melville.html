why i made it

i work at brandy melville in montréal, so i rebuilt it in roblox.

the brand has a huge fan base, and i knew they'd want to walk around and shop in a digital version.

→

building it

  • built the store and layout in roblox studio, scripted in luau
  • normal maps and lighting so it looks like the real thing
  • detailed props modeled in blender (plus an ai mesh tool) from photos
  • so many clothes made it load slow and blurry, so i added an acc loader to load them
→

how it works as a store

try it on

click a piece and it goes on your avatar. mix outfits, take pieces off, check the mirror

fitting rooms

fitting rooms with scripted doors and mirrors all around the store

checkout

buy the pieces you like at the counter, for real
