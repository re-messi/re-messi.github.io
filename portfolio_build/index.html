01 · Featured project

Roblox · 3D retail experience

Melville Clothing

A detailed, explorable fashion retailer inspired by real-world shopping spaces — designed, modeled, merchandised, and shipped as a playable Roblox experience.

70+hours invested
300+visits
250+items sold
20k+social views

The idea

Turning a retail mood into a world.

I wanted the store to feel lived-in rather than game-like: warm wood floors, dense merchandising, collegiate graphics, fitting rooms, baskets, layered lighting, and small details that reward walking around.

The result is a product that sits between game design, retail UX, 3D environment art, and software development.

How I built it
01

Environment design

Designed the storefront, floor plan, fitting-room area, checkout zone, wall systems, and product displays as one cohesive retail environment.

02

3D assets & merchandising

Worked with custom meshes and textures, then staged clothing, baskets, signage, furniture, bags, racks, and props to make the space believable.

03

Lighting & atmosphere

Balanced warm interior lighting, track fixtures, daylight, shadows, and reflective surfaces to create a polished visual identity without losing playability.

04

Interactive product thinking

Built around a real player journey: enter, browse, discover products, try items on, move through the fitting-room area, and purchase clothing.
